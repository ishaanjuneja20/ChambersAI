Does a mouth that moves with the loudness of the voice look acceptable on a photoreal face? Everything runs in your browser: no image or sound is uploaded anywhere.

What to look for

  • Does the chin stretch or the skin smear around the lips? Does the face look like a puppet or a ghost?
  • Is it believable for 5 to 10 seconds of speech, at the size it will be shown in the app?
  • If not: use a few real mouth-shape images swapped by loudness, or a lip-sync service, instead of this.
